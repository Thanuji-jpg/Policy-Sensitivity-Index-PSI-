# Policy-Sensitivity-Index-PSI-
Measuring LP exposure to US trade policy uncertainty across private equity commitment data (2023-2025)

EPUTRADE
Monthly U.S. Trade Policy Uncertainty Index data from FRED (Baker, Bloom & Davis), covering January 1985 through March 2026. The index measures how often major newspapers reference uncertainty around trade policy — higher values mean more uncertainty. Notable spikes appear around the 2018–2019 U.S.–China trade war and the tariff escalations of 2025, where the index exceeded 3,000.

What Is the EPU-TRADE Index?
The EPU-TRADE index is a component of the broader Economic Policy Uncertainty (EPU) framework. It is constructed by searching major U.S. newspapers for articles containing terms related to:

Economy / Economic
Policy / Political
Uncertainty / Uncertain
Trade-specific keywords (tariffs, trade war, WTO, NAFTA, etc.)

The index is normalized so that a value of 100 represents the average level of trade policy uncertainty from 1985–2009. Values significantly above 100 reflect elevated uncertainty relative to that baseline.
